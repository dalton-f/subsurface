# SUBSURFACE

Mars Research Station Ares-7 has been operating for 11 years. 

Originally built for geological research, the discovery of commercially valuable deposits beneath the station transformed Ares-7 into a combined research and mining operation. 

SUBSURFACE puts you in the role of a miner sent deep beneath the station to extract as much valuable ore as possible before time runs out.

## About

SUBSURFACE is a 3D mining roguelike made in Godot, inspired by the exploration and mining of REx Reincarnated and the progression and risk/reward gameplay of Risk of Rain 2.
